# TCL Electronics — Promoter Training System Framework
**Region:** KSA | **Owner:** Country Trainer | **Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** June 2026

---

## 1. System Overview

A structured, repeatable training system to build promoter product knowledge, selling skills, and competitive excellence across TCL product lines.

```
LEARN  →  ASSESS  →  COMPETE  →  RANK  →  REWARD
```

## 2. Program Structure

### 2.1 Training Phases

| Phase | Name | Duration | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 | Foundation | 2 weeks | Product specs, features, USPs |
| Phase 2 | Application | 2 weeks | Selling technique, objection handling |
| Phase 3 | Mastery | 2 weeks | Live demos, competition performance |
| Phase 4 | Champions | Ongoing | Peer coaching, advanced product lines |

### 2.2 Monthly Training Cycle

```
Week 1–2   → Content delivery + self-study
Week 3     → Quiz assessment
Week 4     → Competition / performance evaluation
End of month → Results, ranking, awards
```

## 3. Product Knowledge Quizzes

### 3.1 Quiz Structure

Each product line has **3 quiz levels**:

| Level | Questions | Pass Score | Retake |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bronze | 10 MCQ | 70% | Unlimited |
| Silver | 15 MCQ + 2 scenario | 75% | Max 2× |
| Gold | 20 MCQ + 3 scenario + 1 demo | 85% | Max 1× |

### 3.2 Question Categories

1. **Specs & Features** — panel tech, resolution, ports, sound
2. **Competitive Comparison** — vs. Samsung, LG, Sony at same price point
3. **Customer Scenarios** — "A customer says the price is too high…"
4. **USP Selling Points** — what makes TCL C7L the best choice at its tier

### 3.3 Sample Question Bank (C7L)

```
Q1. What panel technology does the TCL C7L use?
    a) QLED   b) Mini-LED ✓   c) OLED   d) VA

Q2. A customer is comparing C7L to a Sony X90L at the same price.
    What is your strongest counter-argument?
    [Open scenario — scored by trainer on 3-point rubric]

Q3. The C7L supports which gaming feature?
    a) 120Hz VRR ✓   b) 60Hz only   c) 144Hz   d) No gaming mode
```

### 3.4 Quiz Delivery Options

| Method | Tool | When to Use |
|---|---|---|
| In-person | Printed sheets + answer key | Small teams, no connectivity |
| Digital form | Google Forms / Microsoft Forms | Remote or multi-location |
| WhatsApp bot | Typebot / ManyChat | Field promoters on the go |
| LMS platform | Moodle / TalentLMS | Scalable long-term solution |

## 5. Competition Management

### 5.1 Competition Calendar (Annual)

| Competition | Month | Product Focus | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| C7L Mastery — Phase 1 | May | TCL C7L | Video submission |
| C7L Mastery — Phase 2 | July | TCL C7L | Live demo |
| New Product Launch Challenge | October | New lineup | Presentation |
| Year-End Champions Cup | December | All products | Multi-round |

### 5.2 Competition Phases

**Phase 1 — Qualification (Video Submission)**
- Promoter records a 3–5 min product pitch video
- Scored by trainer on standardized rubric
- Top 50% advance to Phase 2

**Phase 2 — Semi-Final (Live Demo)**
- Promoter demos product to a mystery shopper or panel
- Scored live on rubric
- Top 3 awarded

**Phase 3 — Grand Final (optional, annual)**
- Regional finalists compete at a central event
- Brand presence, senior management attendance

### 5.3 Judging Rubric (Standard)

| Criteria | Weight | 1 (Poor) | 2 (OK) | 3 (Good) | 4 (Excellent) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Product Knowledge | 35% | Many errors | Some gaps | Mostly correct | Fully accurate |
| Creativity | 25% | Generic | Minor effort | Engaging | Outstanding |
| Selling Skills | 40% | No technique | Basic pitch | Clear pitch | Full AIDA close |

**Max score: 16 pts × weights = 100%**

### 5.4 Competition Workflow

```
1. Announce competition → Brief all promoters → Share rubric
2. Submission deadline → Collect entries (video link / in-person)
3. Trainer reviews → Score each entry using rubric
4. Calculate rankings → Verify with management
5. Announce winners → Official channel (WhatsApp group / email)
6. Deliver prizes → Finance processes within 2 weeks
7. Share best entry → Use as benchmark for next phase
8. Debrief → Collect feedback → Plan next competition
```
