SQD vs The Competition

QLED

Panel-level quantum dot filter.
Good color. Limited precision.
One filter for millions of pixels.

OLED

Self-emissive pixels.
Great black levels.
Lower peak brightness. Burn-in risk.

SQD

Sub-pixel level filter.
Peak brightness of MiniLED.
Color precision per pixel.
